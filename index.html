About & Engineering Workflow

I am a Computer Science student and Machine Learning Intern at FlyRank specializing in search intelligence, data pipelines, and decision-support ML models. My work centers on turning massive, noisy web datasets into empirical, actionable queues for domain experts.

Core Engineering Principles:

  • ✓ Strict Leakage Prevention: I audit feature matrices rigorously, excluding label-derived fields (e.g. trend_direction, trend_pct) and pseudonymous IDs to guarantee clean out-of-sample evaluation.
  • ✓ Decision-First Framing: Machine Learning is only useful when tied to a specific human decision. I frame models around precision@K and decision costs rather than abstract loss functions.
  • ✓ AI Fluency & Grounded Workflows: I apply human-in-the-loop task classification frameworks and multi-step prompt chains with mandatory adversarial review steps.
