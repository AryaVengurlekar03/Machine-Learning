# Week 9 — Plan to Keep Building

## Where the next case study goes

The next case study will be added to the existing portfolio under the Projects / Case Studies section.

I will reuse the Week 2 three-beat structure:

1. Problem — What real problem was I trying to solve, and why did it matter?
2. What I did — The data, technical approach, tools, experiments, and important design decisions.
3. What came of it — Results, evidence, limitations, and what I would improve next.

## Steps to add the next case

1. Create or identify the completed project/repository.
2. Write the problem in 2–3 sentences.
3. Document the technical approach and most important design decision.
4. Record the measurable result and one honest limitation.
5. Add real screenshots or other evidence where useful.
6. Add the case study to the portfolio Projects / Case Studies section.
7. Add links to the repository, demo, paper, or live application.
8. Test the new case study on desktop and mobile.
9. Commit and push the changes.
10. Verify the live portfolio after deployment.
